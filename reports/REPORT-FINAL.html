v13 is the best coach.

Four architectures built the same live sales coach. One clearly won. Switch below to compare any of them on the complete call, line by line.

  • It quotes the buyer. When it speaks, its advice carries the exact number, name or date the buyer just said. The others answer in generalities that could belong to any sales call.
  • It knows when not to speak. On this whole call it spoke three times and stayed quiet everywhere else. The first attempt interrupted nine times, on product questions, demo talk, even the goodbye.
  • It catches the seller's own mistakes. On harder calls, where the seller talks over the buyer or lets a valuable introduction die, it points at the mistake and says what to do instead. The other coach never ran those calls.
  • It answers in about a second, so it can act mid-call, and the system itself blocks repeat advice, so it never nags.
